## The problem

I take a screenshot to deal with something later — a QR code, a booking, a phone number, a link I don't want to lose. Later never comes. The photo library fills up with a thousand of them, and the thing I actually wanted is buried in an image I now have to scroll to find. The screenshot is a note nobody can read.

## What I built

Shot Done treats screenshots as an inbox instead of a pile. It reads what's in each one and turns it back into something you can act on: open the QR code or link, copy a code, call the number, drop the address into Maps, or save it as an action for later.

Everything lands in a searchable library. You file things into collections, and on-device smart collections pull out the obvious groups on their own — receipts, tickets, travel, products, articles. The backlog gets cleared one screenshot at a time: act, keep, delete, or skip. Nothing is ever deleted quietly; deletion only happens after you choose it and iOS confirms it.

All of the understanding happens on the device. There's no account, no tracking, and no screenshot or extracted text leaves the phone.

Swift throughout, with SQLite as the local store — it ships for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision.
