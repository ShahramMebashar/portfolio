## The problem

Choosing where to study in the Kurdistan Region means piecing together scattered information — a university's own website, a Facebook post, whatever a relative heard. Tuition, language of instruction, program length, and accreditation status are hard to find and harder to compare. Students and parents end up making a decision worth years and thousands of dollars on incomplete information.

## What I built

EDRA puts private universities, colleges and institutes in one place and makes them comparable: search across programs, filter by city, degree, language, and tuition, and put institutions side by side. Every record runs against a published standards framework — verification status, accreditation, and data completeness are enforced by the system rather than negotiated case by case.

It's a Laravel back end with an Inertia and React front end in TypeScript, server-rendered so pages arrive complete and are indexable. PostgreSQL holds the data, with PgBouncer pooling connections, and PM2 runs the SSR process. The whole thing is trilingual — English, Kurdish, and Arabic, with the Kurdish and Arabic sides fully right-to-left.
